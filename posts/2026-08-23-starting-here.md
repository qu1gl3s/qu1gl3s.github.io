There is something useful about having a place of your own on the internet. Not a feed to keep up with, or an audience to perform for—just a place to think in public.

This site is my new notebook for ideas in progress, things I have learned, questions I am still turning over, and the occasional useful detour.

## What belongs here

I expect the subject matter to move around. Technology, work, creativity, and whatever else proves interesting are all fair game. Some entries will be essays. Others might only be a paragraph, a list, or a record of something worth remembering.

The aim is not to arrive with everything figured out. It is to publish often enough that the thinking has somewhere to go.

## A working rule

> Clear enough to share beats perfect enough to postpone.

That feels like a good place to begin.
